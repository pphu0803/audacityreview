# Quiet Abstraction, Riso Voice — Cover Art Philosophy

Cover art for AUDACITY REVIEW behaves like the contribution art of an independent political magazine: abstract, restrained, and quietly argumentative. The image is the article's central metaphor compressed into form — not decoration. The technique anchor is **risograph / screen print**: every cover should look like it was pulled from a small print workshop, not rendered by software.

## Core Principles

1. **One idea per image.** Each cover distills one article into one abstract form. If the form cannot be explained in a sentence, it is too complicated.

2. **Abstract and non-figurative.** Geometric or gestural abstraction only. No faces, no bodies, no objects, no buildings, no charts, no text, no logos. The form suggests; it never depicts.

3. **Two spot inks + paper.** The palette is a riso palette: two muted spot ink colors plus the cream/bone paper as the third "color." Inks are adjacent in value — dusty blues, faded ochres, muted clays, warm greys. Low contrast throughout; the extremes are charcoal and bone, never black and white. Where the two inks overlap, the overprint creates a slightly deeper third tone. This layered flatness is what saves simple shapes from looking empty.

4. **Riso texture is mandatory.** Visible ink grain, matte flat ink, hand-cut imperfect edges, and one or two millimeters of gentle misregistration (soft doubled edges, slight echo offsets). The texture is the style: it is what makes minimalism read as crafted rather than unfinished. No gloss, no gradients, no glow, no 3D.

5. **Clean composition, generous emptiness.** At least half the frame breathes. Cream paper with fine fiber texture visible in the empty areas.

6. **4:3, print finish.** The proportion of the printed page.

## Available Vocabulary

The old "Threshold Minimalism" grammar (mandatory horizontal incision + red hairline) is retired. Available forms, chosen per article: strata and bands, grids, stepped profiles, rows of marks, single gestural strokes, halftone dissolves, quiet voids. One form per cover.

## Prompt Template

When generating, always state: ① "Two-color risograph print, cover art for a political commentary magazine" ② the two named spot inks (muted, adjacent in value) ③ the abstract form and its one-sentence metaphor ④ riso texture cues (ink grain, matte flat ink, hand-cut edges, slight misregistration, overprint third tone) ⑤ constraints (no figures, no objects, no text, low contrast, generous negative space, cream paper) ⑥ 4:3.
